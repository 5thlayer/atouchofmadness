# A disc's freshness is remembered per player

A jukebox restores Sanity, but only rotating at least three discs keeps that restore going. A disc is a Fresh disc unless it is one of the last two discs the player heard. A Stale disc restores half what its last play did, so one disc on repeat, or two discs alternating, fade to nothing.

**Decision.** The history of discs heard belongs to the player and is saved with them. It covers every jukebox, and only the nearest jukebox that is playing counts, so jukeboxes never stack. A hopper that feeds three discs into one jukebox is a legitimate music room.

**Considered.** History kept per jukebox: cheaper, but two jukeboxes each playing one disc in turn would get around the rule.
