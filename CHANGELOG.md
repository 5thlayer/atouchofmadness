# Changelog

Written for Consumers: what a mod building against A Touch of Madness can use, or will see change. A published version never changes: a fix is the next patch (`docs/agents/releases.md`).

## Unreleased

- The Pillar has its own creative tab, "A Touch of Madness", and no longer nests `libworks-runtime` or fills its Works tab (#2).
- Every player has Sanity, 0 to 100: 100 when new, 50 after respawning, saved with the player. It is Sane above 50, Uneasy from 50 down to 20 and Insane below 20, all four set in `atouchofmadness-common.toml`. `/sanity get|set|add <player> [value]`, at permission level 2, reads and moves it, and returns it rounded down for `execute store` (#3).
- Where a player stands drains their Sanity: 6/min in Low light, light at the eyes below 5 unless a full moon is in the sky they can see, in a dimension with a moon; 4/min while freezing, from powder snow or any other cold; 1.5/min in water or rain; and 1/min in a dimension type in `#atouchofmadness:unsettling`, the Nether by default. Every rate and the Low-light threshold are in the `environment` section of `atouchofmadness-common.toml` (#4).
- Eating moves Sanity by the food's Sanity Modifier, the `atouchofmadness:sanity_modifier` item data map, which a datapack can add to or override: −10 for a spider eye or pufferfish down to +30 for an enchanted golden apple, and 0 for any food it doesn't list. Each slice of a placed cake applies the cake's. Waking from sleeping through the night restores 40 to each sleeper, and breaking a block in `#minecraft:flowers` outside creative restores 1; both are in the `moments` section of `atouchofmadness-common.toml` (#6).
- Who is near moves Sanity. Each entity in `#atouchofmadness:unsettling`, vanilla's monsters by default, within 8 blocks drains its weight a minute, from the `atouchofmadness:unsettling_weight` entity-type data map: 1 for a type it doesn't list, 3 for an Enderman, and 10 for the Warden, the Wither and the Elder Guardian, up to 10/min in all. Company restores 2/min, however many are near: any entity in `#atouchofmadness:comforting` within 8 blocks (tamed if it can be tamed; wolves, cats, parrots, horses, donkeys, mules, llamas, nautiluses, villagers and allays by default), or any other player in Survival or Adventure. A datapack can change both tags and the data map. The rates, the radii and the cap are in the `nearby` section of `atouchofmadness-common.toml` (#7).
- Music restores Sanity: while the nearest jukebox playing within 64 blocks plays a disc, 4/min, hopper-fed or not; jukeboxes never stack. A disc that is one of the last two a player heard is Stale and restores half what its last play did, so one disc on repeat gives 4, 2, 1… and two alternating 4, 4, 2, 2…, while three or more in rotation stay at 4. The history is each player's own, saved with them through relogs and death, and shared by every jukebox. The rate and the range are in the `music` section of `atouchofmadness-common.toml` (#8).
- The Flower Crown, the Pillar's first item, in its creative tab: shaped from six flowers of any kind in `#minecraft:flowers`, `F F F` over `F _ F`. Worn in the head slot, with no armour, it restores 1.5/min, and it wears only while worn, shown as a durability bar, until after 1 in-game day of wear it wilts away. Taking hits doesn't wear it, a creative player's never wears, and no anvil, grindstone or crafting repair mends it. The restore and the wear time, in in-game days, are in the `crown` section of `atouchofmadness-common.toml` (#9).
- Discovery: a player learns how Sanity works by meeting it. Each drain, restore and stage below Sane is a hidden advancement in an "A Touch of Madness" tab, done with a toast the first time it actually moves the player's Sanity (a restore at full or a drain at empty never counts) or, for a stage, the first time they reach it; the root, granted on join, explains the meter. Each sits at `atouchofmadness:discovery/<id>`: a pack removes one to count it as known, or overrides it to fire on `minecraft:tick` to mark it known from the start. Two criterion triggers fire them, `atouchofmadness:sanity_changed` with a `source` and `atouchofmadness:stage_reached` with a `stage`. `discovery.enabled` in `atouchofmadness-common.toml`, off, counts everything as known while the advancements are still earned (#18).
- Shadow Creatures, the Lurker and the Gaunt, and Umbra, which is in the creative tab. Around a player Uneasy or Insane in Survival or Adventure, in a dimension type in `#atouchofmadness:shadows` (all three by default), a spawn attempt comes every 20 s while they are under their cap: 1 at 50 Sanity, 2 at 35, 3 at 20 and 4 at 10 and below, the two kinds together, Gaunts only below 10. A creature appears 12 to 24 blocks away on a block it can stand on, out of sight where it can, whatever the light; the attempts stop while `doMobSpawning` is off. Each has one Owner, and only its Owner and Insane players are sent it or can hit it. While the Owner is Uneasy it is Unreal: harmless, unharmable and without collision, stalking at the edge of sight and fading when approached within 4 blocks or struck. While they are Insane it is Real: the Lurker, slow, with 30 health and 4 damage, and the Gaunt, fast, with 16 health and 7 damage; each can be killed for 1–2 or 2–4 Umbra, and keeps to vanilla's difficulty scaling, so on Peaceful it deals nothing yet stays. It is never saved, and goes when its Owner logs out, dies, changes dimension, sleeps or leaves Survival and Adventure, or when their cap falls below their count; it never stops them sleeping. Their Owner's own creatures nearby drain them as `shadow_creature`, apart from `hostile`: by their `atouchofmadness:unsettling_weight` entries, 2 for the Lurker and 4 for the Gaunt, twice that while Real. Each Real hit takes 5 Sanity as `shadow_strike`, and carrying any Umbra drains 1/min as `umbra`; each has a discovery advancement. The caps, interval, distances, Gaunt threshold and chance, Real factor, strike, Umbra drain and each creature's stats are in the `shadows` section of `atouchofmadness-common.toml` (#10).
- Shadow Creatures pass through blocks, as a vex does: both the Lurker and the Gaunt drift with no gravity straight to where they're going, through walls and floors, at 2.5 and 5 blocks a second, `drift` in their `shadows` config sections. One inside a solid block is never hurt by it and lands no hit there: within reach of its Owner, it comes out beside them to strike (#21).
- Killing a Real Shadow Creature restores the killer's Sanity at once as `shadow_kill`, 10 for a Lurker and 20 for a Gaunt, `kill` in their `shadows` config sections. The kill is the player's whose attack, arrows and other projectiles included, dealt the killing blow: the Owner or another Insane player, the only ones who can hit it, and never the Owner of a creature another killed. A creature that fades, vanishes or dies with no player behind the blow restores nothing. It has a discovery advancement under `shadow_creature` (#20).
- A Shadow Creature drops Umbra only when a player gets the kill, by the loot tables' `minecraft:killed_by_player` condition: a player who hurt it in the last 5 s, arrows and their tamed animals included. One that dies of `/kill`, the void, lava or an untamed mob drops nothing, Real or Unreal, unless a player hurt it in the last 5 s (#22).
